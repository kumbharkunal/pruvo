# PRODUCT.md

## What Pruvo is

An independent car inspection service in India ("Professional eyes. Better buys."). Two services, in Pruvo's own names:

- **Pre-delivery inspection (PDI)** for new cars, before the buyer accepts delivery.
- **Pre-purchase inspection** for pre-owned cars, before the buyer pays the seller.

An engineer inspects the car, scans its computer for stored fault codes, and gives the buyer a report with photos. Bookings come in on WhatsApp (+91 93552 17180) and through a Google Form.

## Who the page is for

- A buyer who has a delivery date for a new car and has heard that dealers deliver scratched, repainted or old stock.
- A buyer about to pay for a used car who can't tell whether it has been in an accident, flooded, or had its fault codes cleared.
- Most arrive on a mid-range Android phone from a WhatsApp or Instagram link, often on mobile data.

## The page's job

1. In the first screen: say what Pruvo does and make the buyer curious (the scan).
2. Show the deliverable before asking for anything: what gets checked and what the report looks like.
3. Turn the visit into a WhatsApp booking in under a minute, with a phone call and the Google Form as fallbacks.

## Voice

- Plain, calm, on the buyer's side. "We work for you, not the seller."
- Short sentences, sentence case, no hype and no fear-selling.
- Reuse Pruvo's own lines where they exist ("A car may look perfect on the outside, but what matters is what you can't see.").
- Speak to Indian buyers in Indian English: Indian digit grouping (1,23,456 km), RC, PDI, dealer, token amount.

## Principles

- **Proof over promises.** Readings, photos and a verdict instead of adjectives.
- **Honest by default.** Every number on the page is either Pruvo's own claim or labelled illustrative. Unconfirmed details are flagged for Pruvo to confirm before launch.
- **Fast on a phone.** The first screen works before any JavaScript runs; motion is decoration on top of readable content.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard and screen-reader paths for every interaction, reduced motion respected, statuses never shown by colour alone.

## Out of scope for this demo

Prices, city lists, customer reviews, partner logos, a business address and an online payment flow. These need Pruvo's input first.
